Tokenizer Efficiency Across Languages

Python · Hugging Face Transformers · tiktoken · FLORES-200 · Jupyter · pandas · matplotlib

Measured how efficiently 6 tokenizers (GPT-4, GPT-4o, Llama-3, Mistral, mBERT, NLLB-200) encode 5 languages using FLORES-200 parallel data, prompted by noticing how badly AI tools handle Mongolian, my native second language. Found Mongolian was the least efficiently tokenized language across every model tested (up to 3.74x more tokens than English for identical content) and that the gap is far smaller in tokenizers purpose-built for multilingual coverage (NLLB-200) than general-purpose ones, suggesting it's a solvable design choice rather than an inherent limit.
